Concept Map · Draft

Alexicon 2.0

Seven domains of epistemic governance. Each one holds a question, and guards against a specific way reasoning fails when that question goes unasked.

Foundation

Core Principle

Judgment is cultivated under conditions, not assembled from parts. The architecture designs the conditions.

The Seven Domains
